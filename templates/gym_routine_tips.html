How to Start a Gym Routine

Starting at the gym can be intimidating, but here are some tips to help you get started:

  • Begin with a simple plan and focus on consistency.
  • Learn proper form to avoid injuries.
  • Track your progress to stay motivated.
  • Incorporate rest days for recovery.
  • Fuel your body with nutritious meals.
