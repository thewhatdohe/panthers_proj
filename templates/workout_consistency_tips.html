Staying Consistent with Workouts

Consistency is key to achieving your fitness goals. Here are some tips to help you stay on track:

  • Set realistic and achievable goals.
  • Create a workout schedule and stick to it.
  • Find a workout buddy for accountability.
  • Celebrate small milestones to stay motivated.
  • Mix up your routine to keep it interesting.
